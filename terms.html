2. What the program is

Get Charged Up is a season-long Tampa Bay Lightning pick’em game played among a small, closed group of invited friends and family. Before each Lightning game, each participant picks one Lightning skater. Points are awarded based on what that skater does in the game. A running leader board is kept for bragging rights only.

The program sends you only the messages needed to play:

  • a welcome message when you join, with the rules and command list;
  • a pick invitation before each Lightning game, listing the available skaters;
  • pick reminders if you have not yet made a pick for the open game;
  • a line-up summary at puck drop showing everyone’s picks;
  • a recap after the game with scoring and the updated leader board;
  • replies to commands you send us, such as the roster or the match-up;
  • occasional roster alerts when a player you picked is ruled out.

We do not send marketing, promotions, advertising, solicitations, political messages, charitable appeals or third-party content of any kind. The full rules of the game are on the Overview & Rules page.

Message frequency varies and follows the Tampa Bay Lightning schedule. A typical game generates 3 to 6 messages (one invitation, up to a few daily reminders that stop as soon as you pick, a puck-drop summary and a recap). Replying with a pick stops that game’s reminders immediately.

8. How to stop messages

Opting out

Reply STOP to any text message to cancel. END, QUIT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE and OPT-OUT work the same way.

You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and then no further text messages will be sent to that number.

By email: reply OPT-OUT to any Get Charged Up email, or write to ai.theboltslife@gmail.com with “OPT-OUT” in the subject.

Each channel is stopped separately, so texting STOP ends the texts and leaves email alone, and the reverse. To leave the game entirely, say so in an email to ai.theboltslife@gmail.com and we will remove you from both and delete your record. Opting out takes effect immediately and costs you nothing; no reason is needed and none will be asked for.

To start again after opting out, reply START from the same number, or email us. Nothing will be sent to a number that has opted out until you do.
