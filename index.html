Get Charged Up is a private, season-long Tampa Bay Lightning pick’em game run by Stephen Sullivan, Sole Proprietor for friends and family. These are the public documents for its messaging program.

The short version

Nothing is sold, bought, wagered or won. No fee is charged and no prize is awarded. Messages are sent only to people who asked for them, and stop the moment you reply STOP. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply.

Questions of any kind go to ai.theboltslife@gmail.com.
