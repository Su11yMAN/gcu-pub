Voluntary Participation

Opt in to receive Get Charged Up messages, and how to stop them at any time.

Effective September 14, 2026 · Participation is entirely voluntary

Get Charged Up is invitation-only. If Stephen has invited you to play, this is where you say yes. There are two separate agreements below: the first is required to play and covers the game terms and the game emails; the second is entirely optional and covers text messages. You can say yes to the first and no to the second, and you can withdraw either one at any moment, from any message, for no reason at all.

Before you fill this in
  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Enrol only your own email address, and only your own mobile number if you choose to add one.
  • Nothing is bought, sold, wagered or won. Read the Overview & Rules to see what the game actually is.
  • Taking part is optional in every direction: you are not required to join, you are not required to pick in any given game, and leaving costs nothing.

How to stop — at any time

Opting out

By text: reply STOP to any Get Charged Up message. END, QUIT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE and OPT-OUT do the same thing. You will get one final message confirming you are unsubscribed, and then nothing further.

By email: reply OPT-OUT to any Get Charged Up email, or send a message to ai.theboltslife@gmail.com with “OPT-OUT” as the subject.

To leave completely: email ai.theboltslife@gmail.com and ask to be removed. Your record is deleted; see the Privacy Policy for exactly what that means.

Text and email are switched off separately, so STOP by text ends the texts and leaves the emails running. Opting out is immediate, free, needs no reason, and can be undone by replying START from the same number whenever you like.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any text message for a short message telling you what the program is and how to stop it. For anything a keyword cannot answer, email ai.theboltslife@gmail.com — Stephen reads it himself. Reply WELCOME at any time to have the rules re-sent to you.
